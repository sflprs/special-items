The day was spent in the lower part of the yard. By evening the fires had
Spread so rapidly it looked as if we would have to move before morning...
The grown folks were up and down all night, watching the progress of the
fire.... At half-past ten [the next morning] we were ordered out by the
authorities, as dynamiting was to be done on Van Ness Avenue.

Where should we go with one hundred forty-five
children for shelter? Just here the providence of
God was shown. I was making for the Park when
a soldier, assigned to duty in that neighborhood,
said, “Take your children to the Presidio; you
will be better cared for there.” So to the Presidio
we started. On our way out a gentleman gave us
a large box of crackers. The children sat down by
the wayside to eat this lunch and some ladies
supplied them with good, pure water...

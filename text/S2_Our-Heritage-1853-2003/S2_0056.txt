The Ladies’ Home for children on Franklin Street closes its doors.

The Society celebrates its centennial.

The San Francisco Ladies’ Protection and Relief Society and the Crocker Old People’s Home plan a merger. Architect Mr. Warren Perry
begins plans to enlarge the home on Laguna Street to accommodate residents of both homes.

Former residents of the Crocker Old People’s Home move in and become part of the family at the Society’s home on
Laguna Street. The Laguna Street home becomes certified as a Life Care institution.

The enlarged home on Laguna Street is named THE HERITAGE.

A Health Center is built at The Heritage.

The Heritage establishes the Chinatown Kitchen meal program in collaboration with Self Help for the Elderly.
The Health Center is substantially remodeled and Friendship Hall is built.

A mojor earthquake hits northern California, but The Heritage emerges almost untouched.

The Heritage dining room is remodeled.
